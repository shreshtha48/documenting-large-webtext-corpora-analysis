## 1. Kaggle notebook (Arm A, Table 7)

**Status (2026-10-23):** K3–K6 and K8 fixed and tested; K1 measured (it is real, see EXPERIMENTS.md A1);
K2 (11B) dropped because it does not fit free Kaggle / Colab; K7 and K9 are reported only. Details in EXPERIMENTS.md, "Audit fixes".

Verified correct, with no action needed:
- Credit logic (`long_credits`): negative question → the group not picked gets the credit; positive question →
  the group picked gets it. This matches A.7.
- `pick = sign(Δ)·0.5 + 0.5` maps to subj0 correctly, and ties get 0.5.
- The scorer masks padded label tokens correctly, includes `</s>`, and reuses the encoder output.
- The question generator is deterministic. I read UnQover's `lists.py` and the generator: they use no sets, and
  ordering comes from the files. This gives 147,000 contexts, 14 templates × 10,500 each. subj0 always appears
  first in the context. Each negative stereotype is aligned with its positive counterpart for all 50 attributes
  (I checked them by eye).
